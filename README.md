# 🏥 Health Link

**Health Link** is a digital health records infrastructure platform designed to streamline access to personal healthcare data and services across African countries. The platform empowers individuals with secure access to their medical information while helping clinics and health practitioners offer better, more connected care.

## 🌍 Live Site

🔗 [View Health Link Live](https://healthlink-za.netlify.app/) 

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🆔 My MediCard**  
  A personalized digital health ID for patients to store and manage their medical history securely.

- **📚 Education Portal**  
  A reliable space to access health education materials, awareness campaigns, and preventative care information.

- **📅 My Appointments**  
  View, manage, and track your medical appointments with doctors and health facilities.

- **📍 Clinics Near Me**  
  Location-based tool that helps users find the nearest health clinics and facilities using geolocation.
